These tools are a collection of small scripts, shortcuts and utilities
which can be used standalone or along macros and other buttons. Some
of the tools found within the menus are also available as independent
buttons with tweaks for specific use-cases.

The menu is created dynamically according to your settings, with the
possibility to hide/show specific submenus and tools. It's also possible
to add your own presets as menu entries in some cases (check the menu
entry 'Edit entries from list' whenever applicable).

Shortcuts:
-----------
• L. Click to open menu.
• Ctrl + L. Click to copy the menu entry name without processing.
• Shift + L. Click to switch enabled tools (and submenus).

Tools info:
-----------
Most menus have a small description at the top; further descriptions may
be found at their readme. Their configuration (except presets) may usually
be  found at the global 'Settings' submenu, which also contains the
'Readmes' submenu.

Usage:
------
Menu entries may be grayed (disabled) if some conditions are not met (i.e.
sorting would be disabled when selecting only 1 track), these are related
to some obvious requisites:
	• Tools requiring multiple selected items (>3): those applied to
	a list of tracks.
	• Tools requiring at least 1 item on the active playlist: those
	applied on the entire playlist.
	• Tools requiring a selected/focused item: those applied on
	focused item.

Huge selection lag:
-------------------
Some sub-menus, specially those related to finding/removing tracks on
playlists, may lag at drawing (since the calc. time grows exponentially
to the number of tracks and playlists). To solve that issue some of these
entries may not be created when the number of tracks selected is too high
(user configurable), thus allowing an smooth experience.

A higher limit can be set if desired manually editing the properties panel.
Beware that putting crazy limits, like 10.000 tracks, would render the
entire menu unusable on large selections.
